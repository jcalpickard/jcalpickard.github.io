A simplified form of a language, typically English, Dutch, or Portuguese, some elements of which are taken from local languages, used for communication between those lacking a common language.

A form of lingustic [[bricolage]], a standardised [[protocol]]?

An inherently [[translocality|translocal]] phenomenon, emerging in trading zones and spaces of cultural exchange.
