> FoAM's [Anarchive](https://anarchive.fo.am/) is an [[experimental-publishing]] project recontextualising their 20+ years of transdisciplinary work across art, science, nature, and everyday life. This ongoing process combines archival materials with new commissions through a series of themed fascicles, taking an alternative approach to archives, publishing, and knowledge transmission in complex, turbulent times.

### "Anarchiving"

An experimental practice that approaches archives as living systems, emphasising the activation and recontextualisation of collective knowledge. Moving beyond static preservation, anarchiving takes a fluid, context-sensitive approach to organising shared memories and experiences. Supporting non-linear exploration and emergent connections, it prioritises the generative potential of materials over their fixed historical value, encouraging creative engagement with the past to inform present understanding and future possibilities.
